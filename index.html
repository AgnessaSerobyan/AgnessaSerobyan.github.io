02 / About & how I work

How I work
with a team.

I’m Agnessa, a Senior UX/UI Designer based in Yerevan. I work on mobile and web products, from mapping the flow to reviewing the build with developers.

01

Ask before drawing.

I start by asking what people need to do and what gets in their way. That gives the team something concrete to discuss before we settle on a solution.

02

Design the states, too.

I work through the steps between screens, including partial answers, errors, and feedback. I use shared components for patterns that repeat.

03

Work through the build.

I discuss behaviour and technical constraints with developers, answer questions during implementation, and review the result with them.

03 / AI in practice

Tools I use.
Workflows I share.

I use AI for recurring tasks and turn useful workflows into skills the team can reuse. I write the instructions, connect the steps, and check the results.

How I set it up

Start with a task
worth repeating.

Get in touch
  1. 01

    Write down the task.

    I describe the task, the context it needs, and what the result should look like. The instructions need to make sense to someone using them for the first time.

  2. 02

    Connect the steps.

    I automate the routine parts and leave review points where someone needs to make a decision.

  3. 03

    Check what comes back.

    I check the output against the brief and update the instructions when the task changes or something gets missed.
