01 / Context & design challenge

More than
watching a stream.

Watching a stream does not offer the same ways to join in as attending a festival. We explored how chat, reactions, and visible participants could give people more ways to take part while watching online.

What I looked into

We studied digital communities, live streams, and virtual events, looking at how people talk and react while watching. The audience research covered why people attend online events and when they lose interest.

03 / Interface & visual system

The stage, the chat,
and the people.

The interface pairs dark surfaces with bright green accents. The viewing area, chat, and participant images sit together so the social features stay close to the event itself.

04 / What I delivered

The work
I delivered.

We made an interactive Figma prototype for the online festival concept. It covers choosing a stage, watching, chatting, reacting, and personalising a profile.
