Case 05 / Client MVP · Assistant App

Ghostie✦

A daily assistant,
with a little character.

Ghostie was a one-month client MVP. I designed the app structure, mobile screens, interactions, and visual system for an assistant that helps organise daily tasks and routines.

UX/UI Designer

01 / Context & design challenge

A friendlier way
to manage routines.

The client wanted a routine app that felt personal rather than demanding. My task was to give it a friendly character while keeping everyday actions easy to find.

What I looked into

I reviewed routine, habit, and assistant apps, comparing their flows, language, and visual style. I focused on how the app greets people and asks them to take the next step.

03 / Interface & visual system

A mascot to meet,
a routine to follow.

The mascot gives the app its character. I paired it with lavender surfaces, rounded controls, and generous spacing, keeping the task categories and check-in choices straightforward.

Character & onboarding

The mascot introduces the app during onboarding. I used the same pastel colours in the screens that follow.

Daily structure

The home screen brings the greeting, check-in choices, and routine categories together, rather than spreading the first actions across several screens.

04 / What I delivered

The work
I delivered.

I delivered the MVP design with the app structure, mobile screens, and a visual system built around the mascot and daily routine flow.
