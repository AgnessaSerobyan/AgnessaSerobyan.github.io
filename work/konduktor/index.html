01 / Context & design challenge

More to plan
than the ticket.

A festival trip involves more than buying a ticket. There is transport, somewhere to stay, a budget, and plans around the event. The challenge was to connect these decisions without crowding the mobile interface.

What I looked into

Research included interviews, competitor reviews, journey maps, and empathy maps. We looked at how people choose events and where trip planning becomes difficult, then mapped the steps before, during, and after a visit.

Target audience

People planning a visit to a festival, concert, or exhibition, whether alone, with friends, or with family.
