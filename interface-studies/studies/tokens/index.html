About
this theme editor.

Studio is a fictional product theme. Changes affect only the live preview. The editor keeps its own dark pastel identity.

Publish theme saves a new version in this browser. It does not publish the Site, upload a design system or connect to another service. Reset restores your last published theme.

Both the draft and published version survive reloads. The collection preview is isolated from your working theme. Projects and team members are demonstration data.
