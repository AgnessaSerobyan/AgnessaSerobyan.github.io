Original fictional brand system created for Interface Studies. This is a demonstration guide, not client work.

01 / The wordmark

Use the full wordmark as the primary signature. Keep at least one symbol-width of clear space around it. Do not stretch or rotate the mark.

02 / Colour

#34594f
#d8c5ac
#e1e7d7
#c17858

Deep green is the primary brand colour. Use warm paper as the base and clay for occasional emphasis.

03 / Typography

Aa Bb Cc

Use Arial or a locally licensed sans-serif. Headlines use sentence case and generous spacing. Body text starts at 16px. No font files are bundled.

04 / Composition

Let one message lead. Use broad margins, grouped content and the arch symbol as a recurring motif. Keep the logo separate from busy imagery.
